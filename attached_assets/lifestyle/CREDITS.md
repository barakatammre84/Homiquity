[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free for commercial and non-commercial
use, no permission or attribution required (attribution appreciated). They were downloaded
and committed locally (not hot-linked) so they are served same-origin from the app bundle.

**Representation note (Fair Housing):** this set was curated deliberately for diverse
representation — a range of races, ages, genders, and family compositions — consistent with
the Fair Housing Act §804(c) / HUD guidance on the use of human models in real-estate
advertising. Keep this in mind when swapping any image: replace like-for-like so the overall
set stays representative. There is no company-wide ad-imagery policy yet; flag for compliance
counsel review alongside other public marketing content.
