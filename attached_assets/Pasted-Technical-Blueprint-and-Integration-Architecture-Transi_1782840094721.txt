TRID Date Math and Timeline Reset LogicThe Truth in Lending Act and Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act Integrated Disclosure (TRID) rules govern the timeline for disclosure delivery and loan consummation. The platform’s date-math engine must calculate these timelines using precise regulatory definitions of "business days":The General Definition: Applies to the initial delivery of the Loan Estimate (LE). A business day is any day on which the creditor’s offices are open to the public for carrying on substantially all of its business functions.The Precise Definition: Applies to the delivery of the Closing Disclosure (CD). Under this standard, a business day includes all calendar days except Sundays and the legal federal public holidays specified in 5 U.S.C. 6103(a).The calculation for the earliest legal consummation date $C$ is formulated as:$$C \ge D_{\text{sent}} + T_{\text{delivery}} + W_{\text{waiting}}$$Where:$D_{\text{sent}}$ represents the date the Closing Disclosure is generated and transmitted to the borrower.$T_{\text{delivery}}$ is the delivery transit period. If the CD is delivered in person, $T_{\text{delivery}} = 0$ days. If it is delivered via mail, email, or digital portals, the regulation presumes delivery three business days after transmission ($T_{\text{delivery}} = 3$ precise business days), unless the platform collects auditable digital evidence of earlier receipt. Confirming receipt via E-Sign consent and portal authentication allows $T_{\text{delivery}}$ to be set to the actual date of confirmed electronic receipt.$W_{\text{waiting}}$ is the mandatory waiting period of $3$ precise business days prior to consummation.The calendar engine must evaluate each day sequentially. If a calculation interval includes a Sunday or a federal holiday, the engine must skip that day, extending the timeline by one business day.The table below illustrates the TRID compliance calculations for a standard Closing Disclosure delivery cycle.Step in Date-Math PipelineOption A: Digital E-Sign Delivery & Confirmed ReceiptOption B: Digital Delivery (Presumed Delivery/Mailbox Rule)Calculation and Holiday Exclusion RulesCD Transmission Date ($D_{\text{sent}}$)Monday, Nov 10Monday, Nov 10Initial reference date (Day 0).Presumed Delivery Window ($T_{\text{delivery}}$)Monday, Nov 10 (E-Sign Proof Captured)Tuesday, Nov 11 to Thursday, Nov 13Option B adds 3 precise business days. Under TRID, a new three-business-day waiting period is triggered only if one of the following scenarios occurs:The loan product's program changes (e.g., transitioning from a fixed-rate to an adjustable-rate mortgage).A prepayment penalty is added to the loan terms.The Annual Percentage Rate (APR) becomes inaccurate, varying by more than $1/8$ of a percent ($0.125\%$) for a standard fixed-rate loan, or more than $1/4$ of a percent ($0.25\%$) for an irregular or adjustable-rate loan.
