- "The Mortgage Call Report (MCR) must be completed by all state-licensed companies and
  companies employing state licensed mortgage loan originators (MLOs)." (p.126)
- "Companies holding one or more state licenses or state registrations through NMLS … during a
  calendar quarter are required to complete a Mortgage Call Report (MCR)." (p.127)
- Homiquity holds the Illinois Residential Mortgage License #3423789 (founder-supplied, IDFPR;

- Zero activity does not exempt the filing: the state-specific RMLA is required "even if there
  was no activity during the reporting period." (p.129)

## The two components, and which apply

| Component | Who files | Cadence | Deadline (guidebook) |
|---|---|---|---|
| RMLA — Residential Mortgage Loan Activity | all filers | quarterly | "The MCR is due 45 days from the end of each calendar quarter." (p.126) |
| FC — Financial Condition | brokering companies: annually. (Lending/servicing companies: quarterly, p.126) | annual for brokers | "Companies reporting brokering business activities must submit the Financial Condition (FC) component no later than 90 days from the calendar year end." (p.126) |

- RMLA has a **company-level** part (lines of credit, nationwide servicing — completed once) and
  a **state-specific** part per licensed state (p.128–129). With one IL license: one company-level
  + one IL component.
- The RMLA collects "application, closed loan, individual MLO, Line of Credit, servicing, and
  repurchase information by state." (p.127)
- **Standard vs Expanded:** "Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac Sellers/Servicers and/or Ginnie Mae Issuers
  must submit an Expanded MCR. All other companies are required to submit the Standard MCR."
  (p.127) Homiquity holds no GSE seller/servicer approval (every GSE integration is a
  deterministic simulation; no contract exists), which indicates **Standard** — NMLS determines
  the required filing type on-screen (p.129); confirm what it renders rather than overriding.

## ⚠️ Escalated wording variance (R4 — founder/Resource Center resolves, never harmonized here)

- p.126: brokering companies complete the FC "annually **at the calendar year end**" and file it
  "no later than 90 days from the **calendar year end**."
- p.128: "For Standard filers, the FC is required to be filed annually, no later than 90 days
  after the **fiscal year end**."

For a company whose fiscal year is the calendar year the two readings coincide. If Homiquity's
fiscal year differs, they diverge — resolve on the NMLS Resource Center MCR page (p.128 itself
directs there for filer-classification questions) or with the regulator before relying on either
