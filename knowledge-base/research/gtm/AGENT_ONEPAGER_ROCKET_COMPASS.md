# Agent Partner One-Pager — countering the Rocket/Redfin/Compass ecosystem

**Last updated:** 2026-07-06 · **Use:** real-estate agent outreach (B2B2C channel).
**Status:** internal source copy — external use requires the standard brand/compliance pass.

## The market moment (why agents will take this meeting now)

- 2026-02: Rocket and Compass announced a three-year alliance; Redfin becomes a home-search
  partner for Compass's ~340,000 agents. Rocket ran a joint Super Bowl ad with Redfin.
- Net effect: the largest lender in the country is building a closed loop — search
  (Redfin), agent (Compass), mortgage (Rocket). Buyers who enter it get monetized at every
  step, and the agent's client relationship becomes Rocket's funnel.
- Every agent NOT at Compass now has a concrete reason to want an independent financing
  partner who won't compete for their client.

## The pitch (agent-facing)

**Your buyer stays your buyer.**

1. **We are a broker, not a retail lender with an agent portal.** We shop wholesale lenders
   that compete for your client's loan — we don't route your client into our ecosystem's
   listings, our agents, or our next refi campaign.
2. **The No-Stall promise, agent edition:** you see the same file status the LO sees —
   referral dashboard with live milestones, so you never call a closing you can't predict.
   (Referral dashboard is built and wired.)
3. **Speed where it saves deals:** dual-AUS findings before lender submission; conditions
   chased daily; you learn about problems from us, not from the closing table.
4. **Local depth national lenders can't fake:** we know IHDA, Chicago HomeGrown, and Cook
   County DPA cold — the difference between a marginal buyer walking and closing.
   (Send agents our three Learning Center DPA guides as shareable value.)

## What we will NOT do (say it out loud — it builds trust)

- No referral fees, kickbacks, or things-of-value for referrals — RESPA §8 is absolute.
  What we offer agents is execution and visibility, not payment.
- No marketing to their client for competing services; no listing-side land grab.

## Proof points to lead with

- Live referral dashboard demo (2 minutes, real file statuses)
- The three IL DPA guides as leave-behind content the agent can send buyers today
- Clear-to-close velocity stats — once we have ≥10 closed files; do not use projections

## Compliance rails

RESPA §8: no thing-of-value for referrals; co-marketing (if ever) must be fair-value,
documented, and reviewed first. Reg N/UDAAP: no claims about rates or approval odds in
agent-facing material that a consumer could see. Pre-F1: this document does not leave the
building until licensing clears — the charter's public-claim blocker class applies to any
surface a stranger can reach.
