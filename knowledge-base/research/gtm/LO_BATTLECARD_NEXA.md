# LO Recruiting Battlecard — vs. NEXA Mortgage

**Last updated:** 2026-07-06 · **Use:** recruiting conversations with producing loan officers.
**Review cadence:** quarterly, or immediately after a NEXA comp/tech announcement.

## Their pitch (as of July 2026, from their public recruiting materials)

- "#1 mortgage broker in the country," licensed in 48 states (NY/MA pending)
- 220bps compensation; example math they lead with: ~$79K/yr on one $300K loan/mo
- 250+ wholesale lenders; 300+ processors
- 10bps override on every LO you recruit (the growth engine is recruiting, not support)
- New agentic-AI POS ("Bevri") that automates the 1003 and runs real-time underwriting logic
- Fully remote

## Be honest about their strengths

- Comp is real and high; lender menu is genuinely broad
- For a self-sufficient high-volume LO who wants to be left alone, the model works
- Brand recognition among LOs shopping brokerages

## Their structural weaknesses

- **The override pyramid means the org optimizes for headcount, not per-LO throughput.**
  300 processors across thousands of LOs is thin; support is a queue, not a team.
- AI POS claims are front-of-funnel; the stall points that kill deals (conditions,
  underwriting touch, lender follow-up) are exactly where a thin-support model breaks.
- An LO there is one of thousands — no say in tooling, no leverage on process.

## Our counter-positioning (velocity proof, not tech claims)

1. **No-Stall Guarantee** — we sell the LO what they sell the borrower: files that do not
   sit. NEXA sells basis points; we sell throughput. A stalled file costs an LO more than
   20bps ever pays back.
2. **LO Command Center** (built, PR #33) — live file-level visibility: where every loan is,
   what is blocking it, who owes what. Demo it; do not describe it.
3. **Dual-AUS by default** (DU + LPA leg) — findings both ways before lender submission,
   fewer post-submission surprises.
4. **Small-shop leverage** — direct line to the people who build the platform; process
   changes in days, not roadmap quarters.

## Objection handling

| If the LO says… | Respond with… |
|---|---|
| "NEXA pays 220bps" | "On files that close. What's your stall rate costing you per month? Walk me through your last three files that sat in conditions." |
| "They have 250 lenders" | "How many do you actually use? Our Target-5 catalog covers the pricing you'd shop 95% of the time, with a status machine that chases them — not you." |
| "They have an AI POS now" | "So does everyone by Q4. The question is who works your file when the AI hits a condition it can't clear. Ask them their processor-to-LO ratio." |
| "You're small / new" | "Small is the feature: you're a founding LO with input on the platform, not row 4,000 in a downline." |

## Landmines to set (questions to ask the LO early)

- "What's your average clear-to-close time on your last ten files?"
- "When a file stalls, who do you call — and do they answer?"
- "How much of your recruiter's income comes from your production override?"

## Compliance rails

Comp discussions must respect Reg Z LO compensation rules (12 CFR 1026.36(d)) — no
comp based on loan terms. Do not misstate NEXA's published numbers; cite their own
recruiting materials. Recruiting claims about our velocity must be supportable with
platform data before use in writing.
