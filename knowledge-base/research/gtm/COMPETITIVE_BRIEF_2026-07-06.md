## Executive summary

Every digital competitor tells the same story — speed + AI + low rates — while Rocket buys the
emotional high ground with "Own the Dream." Three positions are genuinely unclaimed:
**certainty** (will I actually qualify and close?), **human judgment for complex income**
(Better's top complaint theme is "assembly line"), and **local down-payment-assistance
expertise** (national lenders can't credibly do it). Homiquity's assets — Approval Strength
concept, the self-employed "humans read your file" page, Illinois licensure, Tax Return
Insight — map onto all three.

- **Biggest opportunity:** IL/Chicago DPA content (IHDAccess Home launched 2026-03; Chicago
  HomeGrown launched 2026-06 with grants to $70K; Cook County DPA reopens **2026-07-20**) plus
  the unclaimed "your tax return is a mortgage document" territory.
- **Biggest threat:** Rocket–Redfin–Compass alliance (2026-02, ~340K agents) closing off the
  agent B2B2C channel.

## Messaging map

Everyone's hero is the machine (Better/Beeline) or the dream (Rocket) or the price (Tomo).
**Homiquity is the only player whose hero is human judgment.** Better's top complaint is our
positioning stated in reverse. Our "3 min / no credit check" chips were word-for-word Better's
CTA — reframed 2026-07-06 to lead with certainty (FTB: "Know exactly where you stand"; SE: "A
real answer, not an algorithm's guess").
